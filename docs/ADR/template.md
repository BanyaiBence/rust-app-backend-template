# NNNN. Title as a short noun phrase describing the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who approved it
- **Supersedes:** [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-title.md) (omit if none)

## Context

What problem are we solving, and why now? Describe the forces at play — requirements,
constraints, quality attributes, team or tooling realities — neutrally, without giving away the
answer. A reader should be able to judge the options from this section alone.

## Considered options

1. **Option A** — one-line description.
   - Pro: …
   - Con: …
2. **Option B** — one-line description.
   - Pro: …
   - Con: …
3. **Do nothing / keep the status quo** — include when it is a real alternative.

## Decision

We chose **Option X**, because … (the deciding reasons, tied back to the forces in the context).

Describe the decision concretely enough to act on: what is done, where, and any rules that
follow from it.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what becomes easier or safer.
- **Negative:** what becomes harder, slower or riskier, and the costs we accept.
- **Follow-up:** work this decision creates, and the signals that would make us revisit it.
